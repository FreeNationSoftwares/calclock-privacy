[calclock] Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

What the app does

[calclock] is a calculator-style private vault. You can hide photos, videos, files and notes behind a PIN.

Your files stay on your device

Files and notes you add are encrypted on your device. We do not upload them to any server and we cannot see them. There is no account or sign-in.

Camera (optional)

If you turn on "Intruder selfie", the app takes a front-camera photo after three wrong PIN attempts. The photo is stored encrypted on your device only and is never uploaded. You can turn this off or delete the photos at any time.

Private browser

The built-in browser clears history, cache and cookies when you leave it. Websites you visit have their own privacy policies. Files you download are saved encrypted inside the vault.

Backups

Backups are created only when you choose, are protected by a password you set, and are saved where you choose (for example Google Drive). We cannot recover a forgotten backup password.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address and usage data to show and measure ads. In the EEA and UK you can manage your ad consent choices in the consent form shown by the app. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact
