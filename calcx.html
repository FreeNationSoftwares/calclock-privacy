CalcX – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

CalcX ("the app") is developed by FreeNation Softwares. CalcX is a calculator that also lets you hide your own private photos, videos, files and notes in a PIN-protected, encrypted vault on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Your files stay on your device

Everything you hide in CalcX — photos, videos, audio, documents and notes — is encrypted and stored only on your own device using Android Keystore (AES-256). Your files, their contents and your PIN are never uploaded to any server and are never collected, stored or shared by us. We have no account system and no access to your vault.

2. Your PIN and recovery

Your PIN, decoy PIN and recovery answer are stored only on your device in encrypted form (only a salted hash is kept). We cannot see or recover them. If you forget both your PIN and your recovery answer, your data cannot be restored.

3. Camera (intruder selfie)

If you turn on the optional "intruder selfie" feature, the app uses the front camera to take a photo after repeated wrong PIN attempts. These photos are saved encrypted on your device only and are never uploaded or shared. The camera is used for no other purpose.

4. Fingerprint / biometrics

If you enable fingerprint unlock, authentication is handled entirely by Android's secure biometric system. The app never receives or stores your fingerprint data.

5. Advertising

The free version of CalcX shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, app usage and crash/diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

6. Permissions

  • Photos / media / files – only to let you pick the files you choose to hide in the vault.
  • Camera – only for the optional intruder-selfie feature; photos stay encrypted on your device.
  • Biometric / fingerprint – only for unlocking the vault, handled by Android.
  • Internet – only to load ads and to load pages in the app's private browser.

7. Private browser

The in-app private browser loads web pages you choose. Its bookmarks and history are stored only inside your encrypted vault on your device. We do not collect your browsing data.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data deletion

We do not hold any of your data. You can delete hidden items inside the app at any time, and uninstalling CalcX removes all app data (including the vault) from your device.
