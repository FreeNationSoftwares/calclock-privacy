Unpass: Unlock PDF Password – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Unpass ("the app") is developed by FreeNationSoftwares. This policy explains what information the app handles.

1. Your PDF files stay on your device

All PDF tools in Unpass (Unlock, Protect, Merge, Split, Compress) work 100% offline on your phone. Your PDF files, their contents and the passwords you type are never uploaded to any server and are never collected, stored or shared by us. Processed files are saved only to your device's Download/Unpass folder.

2. Passwords

Passwords you enter are used only for that single operation in your phone's memory and are not saved by the app.

3. Advertising

The free version of Unpass shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model, app usage and crash/diagnostic data. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

4. Permissions

  • Internet – only to load ads. PDF processing does not need the internet.
  • File access – only the files you choose with the system file picker.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Data deletion

We do not hold any of your data. Files created by the app can be deleted from your Download/Unpass folder at any time. Uninstalling the app removes all app data from your device.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions? Contact us through the developer contact details on the Unpass Google Play Store page.
